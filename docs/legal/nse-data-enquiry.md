# Draft enquiry to NSE Data Services

Send to dataservices@nse.co.ke (phone +254 20 2831000). This is a draft for the team to adapt;
it is not legal advice, and the licence decision needs counsel's sign-off (MVP-20).

**Subject:** End-of-day data licence for a public research website

Dear NSE Data Services team,

We are building a small research website that shows end-of-day prices for NSE-listed equities
and a statistical forecast of each liquid stock's next official closing price, with a
published track record. It will not offer trading, recommendations or real-time data.

Having read the NSE Market Data Policies (version 6/2026), we would like to understand the
licences and fees that apply to:

1. Displaying end-of-day prices, volumes and index levels on a public website, with NSE
   attribution and a disclaimer as set out in section 21.
2. Publishing derived data (our forecasts and accuracy statistics) under section 20.
3. Processing NSE data to train and run forecasting models (non-display use, section 20).
4. Calculating and showing a simple market breadth figure (advancers and decliners) and,
   if needed, an equal-weighted reference index.
5. Licensing historical end-of-day data from 2005 to date for research.

Could you also confirm whether a non-commercial or start-up rate exists, and send the current
price list (the 2023 price list on the website is a scanned image we could not read)?

Kind regards,

[Name, organisation, contact details]
